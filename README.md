# Melophile Records

Independent music label for artists who make you feel something.

Melophile Records is a home for boundary-pushing sounds, honest songwriting, and music made with intention. We work with emerging artists across alternative, electronic, indie, R&B, hip-hop, and everything in between.

## What we do

- Artist development  
- Music distribution  
- Release strategy  
- Creative direction  
- Marketing and community building  

## Our mission

We believe great music deserves a patient, artist-first approach. Melophile Records helps artists build sustainable careers while staying true to their sound.
